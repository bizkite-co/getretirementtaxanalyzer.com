## ⭐ Non‑obvious insight (this is where your tech background helps)

<span class="font-ligatures-none whitespace-pre-wrap">People respond better to **trajectory metaphors** than depletion metaphors.</span>

<span class="font-ligatures-none whitespace-pre-wrap">Burn‑down → feels like loss Trajectory / glidepath → feels like direction, control, and engineering precision</span>

<span class="font-ligatures-none whitespace-pre-wrap">Your table is essentially a **multi‑decade cash‑flow trajectory** with tax‑aware constraints. Framing it as a *flight plan*, *glidepath*, or *roadmap* makes the conversation easier for both advisors and clients.</span>
